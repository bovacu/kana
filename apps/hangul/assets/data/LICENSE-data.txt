Character data (characters.kana)
================================

characters.kana is baked from the works below and is a derived work of them.
It is distributed under their licences, and this attribution must be shown to
the app's users (Settings › Licences).


Hangul's strokes
----------------
The strokes of the jamo and of every syllable, in writing order: drawn for
Hangul (apps/hangul/tools/strokes/), the syllables composed from the jamo by
rule. No outside data.


KanjiVG
-------
Stroke data: the strokes of the hanja, in writing order.

  Copyright (C) 2009-2025 Ulrich Apel.
  KanjiVG is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0
  licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
  Website: https://kanjivg.tagaini.net
  Source used: release r20250816 (kanjivg-20250816.xml.gz). Hangul keeps the
  hanja Korean learners meet, unchanged.


한국어기초사전 (Basic Korean Dictionary)
--------------------------------------
Words: their levels (beginner, intermediate, advanced), pronunciation, hanja
origin, and meanings in English, Spanish, French and Japanese.

  국립국어원 한국어기초사전 (National Institute of Korean Language, Basic Korean
  Dictionary, https://krdict.korean.go.kr), licensed under the Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Korea licence (CC BY-SA 2.0 KR):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/kr/
  Hangul keeps its words (not its phrases, grammar patterns, sounds or
  pictures), with their first meanings shortened, their pronunciation
  written in the Revised Romanization of Korean, and the words whose hanja
  origin has a character listed under that character.
  Source used: its full download (JSON), dated 2026-09-19.

wordfreq
--------
How common each word is.

  wordfreq by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), whose data is

Tatoeba
-------
Example sentences: a Korean sentence for a word, and its English, Spanish,
French and Portuguese translations.

  Sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), the work of its contributors,
  used under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
  Hangul keeps a selection of them (one for each word, chosen by length) and
  changes none.
  Source used: Tatoeba's per-language exports
  (https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/), downloaded 2026-10-03.

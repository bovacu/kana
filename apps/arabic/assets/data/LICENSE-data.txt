Character data (characters.kana)
================================

characters.kana is baked from the works below and is a derived work of them.
It is distributed under their licences, and this attribution must be shown to
the app's users (Settings › Licences).


Arabic's strokes
----------------
The strokes of every letter, in each of the forms it takes in a word, of the
vowel marks and of the digits, in writing order: drawn for Arabic
(apps/arabic/tools/strokes/), in the Naskh hand schools teach. No outside
data. What each letter sounds like and each form is (ث: th, as in think) is
written for Arabic too.


Wiktionary
----------
Words: their vowelled spelling, romanization and meanings in English; meanings
in Spanish, French, Portuguese and Japanese where those Wiktionaries have the
word; the inflected forms that lead a sentence's words to theirs.

  From Wiktionary (https://www.wiktionary.org), the work of its contributors,
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free
  Documentation License; extracted by wiktextract (Tatu Ylönen) and published
  by kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org). Arabic keeps its Arabic words (not
  names, letters, affixes or inflected forms), with their first meanings
  shortened.
  Sources used: kaikki.org's Arabic files from the English, Spanish, Japanese,
  French and Portuguese Wiktionaries (extracted from the dumps of 2026-09-02 to
  2026-10-01), downloaded 2026-10-04.


wordfreq
--------
How common each word and letter is.

  wordfreq by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), whose data is
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).


Tatoeba
-------
Example sentences: an Arabic sentence for a word, and its English, Spanish,
French and Portuguese translations.

  Sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), the work of its contributors,
  used under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
  Arabic keeps a selection of them (one for each word, chosen by length) and
  changes none.
  Source used: Tatoeba's per-language exports
  (https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/), downloaded 2026-10-04.

Character data (characters.kana)
================================

characters.kana is baked from the works below and is a derived work of them.
It is distributed under their licences, and this attribution must be shown to
the app's users (Settings › Licences).


Thai's strokes
--------------
The strokes of every letter, vowel sign, tone mark and digit, in writing
order: drawn for Thai (apps/thai/tools/strokes/), following the school
rules. No outside data. The letters' meanings (ก: chicken) are written for
Thai too.


Wiktionary
----------
Words: their tone romanization and meanings in English; meanings in Japanese,
and in French and Portuguese where Volubilis has none.

  From Wiktionary (https://www.wiktionary.org), the work of its contributors,
  licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free
  Documentation License; extracted by wiktextract (Tatu Ylönen) and published
  by kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org). Thai keeps its Thai words (not
  names, letters or affixes), with their first meanings shortened.
  Sources used: kaikki.org's Thai files from the English, Japanese, French and
  Portuguese Wiktionaries (extracted from the dumps of 2026-09-02 to
  2026-10-01), downloaded 2026-10-04.


Volubilis
---------
Words: their levels (A0, A1, A2) and meanings in Spanish, French and
Portuguese.

  VOLUBILIS MULTILINGUAL THAI DICT. & DATABASE by Belisan (Francis Bastien),
  https://belisan-volubilis.blogspot.com/, licensed under the Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Thai keeps the levels and the first meanings, shortened, without their
  grammar notes.
  Source used: VOLUBILIS Mundo, v. 26.2 (July 2026), downloaded 2026-10-04.


PyThaiNLP
---------
How common each word and letter is.

  The word frequency list of the Thai National Corpus (tnc_freq.txt) from
  PyThaiNLP (https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/pythainlp), released under the
  Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.


Tatoeba
-------
Example sentences: a Thai sentence for a word, and its English, Spanish,
French and Portuguese translations.

  Sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), the work of its contributors,
  used under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
  Thai keeps a selection of them (one for each word, chosen by length) and
  changes none.
  Source used: Tatoeba's per-language exports
  (https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/), downloaded 2026-10-04.

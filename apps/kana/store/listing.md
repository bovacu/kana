# Kana: the App Store listing

What goes into App Store Connect, in Kana's five languages (App Store
localizations: English (U.S.), Spanish (Spain), Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese,
French). Every field fits App Store Connect's limits: name and subtitle 30
characters, promotional text 170, keywords 100, description 4,000. The
keywords leave out the words already in the name and subtitle, because the
App Store indexes those anyway.

Figures are from the app's data (2026-10-02): 6,412 kanji, all the kana, 54,362
words and 14,595 example sentences.

The screenshots and how to upload them are in `README.md`. Privacy, the App
Privacy answers and permissions are in `../docs/app_store.md`. The privacy and
support pages are in `../site/` (`tools/store/site.py`).

  countries' prices from it). No in-app purchases, no subscription, no free
  trial. Join the App Store Small Business Program first: 15% commission
  instead of 30%. The descriptions say "one purchase, everything included".
  If the price model changes, change that line.
- **Age rating:** 12+ chosen (2026-10-02). Apple's ratings are now 4+, 9+, 13+,
  16+ and 18+, so it shows as **13+**: the questionnaire answers below give it.
- **Copyright:** `2026 <your name>` (or the seller name in App Store Connect).
- **Devices:** iPad only (iPadOS 15 or later).
- **Support URL / Privacy Policy URL:** the two pages from `../site/` once
  hosted (`../docs/app_store.md`, "Privacy policy and support pages").
- **Contact for App Review:** rde.apps.support@gmail.com.
- **Age rating questionnaire:** answer for what the app SHOWS. The dictionary
  (JMdict) and the example sentences (Tatoeba) are real Japanese, so a few
  words and sentences touch violence, alcohol and drugs, sex and crude
  language. Honest answers:
  - **Infrequent/Mild:** *Profanity or Crude Humor*, *Mature or Suggestive
    Themes*, *Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References*, *Cartoon or
    Fantasy Violence* and *Realistic Violence*.
  - **None:** everything else.
  - Also: no unrestricted web access, no user-generated content shared, no
    gambling.

  That rates it 13+. A 4+ rating would need those words and sentences filtered
  out of the bake first. That is a choice, not a requirement.
- **What's New:** not shown for version 1.0. Start using it from 1.1.
- **App Review notes** (English):

  > Kana needs no account. It works with a finger or an Apple Pencil: on a new
  > install the hand on the toolbar is on, so one finger writes and two fingers
  > move the page; the first Apple Pencil touch switches to Pencil writing
  > (fingers then move the page), and the hand switches back.
  > To see the main features:
  > (1) The menu at the top left › Kanji, then any character: its stroke order
  > plays, with words and an example sentence; Practice writes it with guidance.
  > (2) On the page, write a few characters, circle them with the lasso, and
  > choose Check, Save word or Translate with Google.

  > (4) The menu › Lectures: open a free lesson, write or highlight on it, circle

  > (5) The menu › Vocabulary: words, lists, word exams, and Translate with
  > Google (type in English, get Japanese).

## English (U.S.)

**Name** (30): Kana: Write Japanese

**Subtitle** (30): Kanji & kana, stroke by stroke

**Promotional text** (170): Write Japanese by hand and Kana reads it. Point the camera at Japanese and translate it. Scan your textbook and write on it. One purchase, no account.

**Keywords** (100): hiragana,katakana,handwriting,jlpt,nihongo,vocabulary,translate,ocr,scanner,dictionary,pencil,study

**Description:**

Write Japanese by hand, and Kana reads it. Point the camera at Japanese, and Kana translates it. Scan your textbook, and write on it.

Kana is a Japanese notebook for your iPad that understands what you write.

WHAT IT DOES
• Reads your handwriting: kanji and kana, written with the Apple Pencil or your finger.
• Translates with Google: what you write, what you photograph, any line of a PDF, and your own language into Japanese.
• Recognizes text with the camera: point it at a sign, a menu or a book, live or from a photo.
• Scans documents: camera scans, PDFs and photos become lessons you can highlight, search and write on.
• Shows every kanji writing itself, all 6,412 of them, with words and example sentences.
• Brings your words back for review right before you would forget them.

WRITE, AND KANA READS IT
• An endless page for notes and practice. Circle what you wrote to check it, copy it as text, save a word or translate it.
• Paste Japanese text, and Kana writes it out by hand on your page.
• Five themes, from washi paper to night.

TRANSLATE, BOTH WAYS
• From the camera: Kana finds the Japanese, translates it line by line, and writes the lines you keep on your page.
• Into Japanese: type a word or a sentence in your language, get it in Japanese, then save its words, write it or practice its characters.
• It all runs on your iPad with Google ML Kit: download it once, then translate offline.

EVERY KANJI, STROKE BY STROKE
• 6,412 kanji and all the hiragana and katakana, each one writing itself in the right order.
• Readings, meanings, parts, stroke counts, school grades and JLPT levels from N5 to N1.
• 54,362 words and 14,595 example sentences, read aloud and translated.
• Can't type a kanji? Draw it, or pick its parts, and Kana finds it.
• Look-alikes show the characters that get mixed up, yours first. Keep your own note on any character.

PRACTICE UNTIL IT STICKS
• Guided practice, from tracing to writing from memory, with points for shape and stroke order.
• Your own vocabulary in lists like "Lesson 3" or "Travel", with word exams: write each word from its meaning, or by ear.
• Reviews, exams by JLPT level, printable practice sheets, statistics, and an album of everything you have written.

READ REAL LESSONS, AND WRITE ON THEM
• Free lessons included: First Year Japanese I (college handouts and worksheets) and a Japanese phrasebook for travelers.
• Bring your own from Files, Photos or the camera's document scanner. Kana reads the Japanese in them, even in scans, so you can search it, select it and translate it.
• Highlight with the marker, write in the margins, jump to a page, turn a scan upright, and share it all as a PDF with your notes.

YOURS, AND PRIVATE
One purchase, everything included. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your pages, words and progress stay on your iPad; export them whenever you like. In English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and French.

Best with an Apple Pencil, and your finger works too.

## Japanese

**名前** (30): Kana: 手で書く日本語

**サブタイトル** (30): 漢字とかなを一画ずつ

**プロモーション用テキスト** (170): 手で書いた日本語をKanaが読み取ります。カメラを向ければ翻訳、教科書をスキャンすればそのまま書き込み。買い切りで、アカウント不要。

**キーワード** (100): ひらがな,カタカナ,書き順,手書き,練習,JLPT,単語帳,復習,翻訳,文字認識,スキャン,辞書,書き取り

**説明:**

手で書けば、Kanaが読む。カメラを向ければ、Kanaが訳す。教科書をスキャンすれば、そのまま書き込める。

Kanaは、書いたものを理解するiPadの日本語ノートです。

できること
• 手書きを読み取る：漢字もかなも、Apple Pencilでも指でも。
• Googleで翻訳：書いたもの、撮ったもの、PDFのどの行も。自分の言葉から日本語へも。
• カメラで文字認識：看板やメニュー、本にかざすだけ。撮った写真からも。
• 書類をスキャン：スキャンしたページ、PDF、写真が、マーカーを引けて、検索できて、書き込める教材に。
• 漢字6,412字すべてが書き順どおりに書かれていく様子を、言葉と例文と一緒に。
• 忘れかけたころに、言葉を復習に出します。

書けば、Kanaが読む
• メモと練習のための無限のページ。書いたものを囲めば、チェック、テキストでコピー、単語の保存、翻訳ができます。
• 日本語の文章を貼り付けると、Kanaが手書きでページに書いてくれます。
• 和紙から夜まで、5つのテーマ。

翻訳は、どちら向きにも
• カメラから：Kanaが日本語を見つけて1行ずつ翻訳し、残したい行をページに書きます。
• 日本語へ：自分の言葉で単語や文を入力すれば日本語に。含まれる単語を保存したり、ページに書いたり、その字を練習したりできます。
• すべてGoogle ML KitによりiPad上で動きます。一度ダウンロードすれば、オフラインで翻訳できます。

どの漢字も、一画ずつ
• 漢字6,412字と、ひらがな・カタカナのすべて。どの字も書き順どおりに書いて見せます。
• 読み、意味、部品、画数、学年、JLPTレベル（N5〜N1）。
• 54,362語と14,595の例文。読み上げと翻訳付き。
• 読めない漢字は、描くか部品を選べば見つかります。
• 似ている字：まぎらわしい字を、自分が間違えた字から順に。どの字にも自分だけのメモを。

身につくまで練習
• なぞり書きから何も見ずに書くまで導く練習。形と書き順に点数が付きます。
• 「第3課」「旅行」など自分のリストで単語帳を。言葉のテストでは、意味から、または聞いて書きます。
• 復習、JLPTレベル別のテスト、練習シートの印刷、統計、そして書いたすべての字のアルバム。

本物の教材を読んで、書き込む
• 無料の教材付き：大学の日本語コースの補助教材とワークシート「First Year Japanese I」（英語）と、Wikivoyageの日本語フレーズ集（英語）。
• 自分の教材も：ファイル、写真、またはカメラの書類スキャンから。スキャンした中の日本語もKanaが読み取るので、検索も選択も翻訳もできます。
• マーカーを引き、余白に書き込み、ページへ移動し、スキャンの向きを直して、メモごとPDFで共有。

あなたのものを、あなたの手元に
買い切りで、すべての機能が使えます。アカウントも広告もトラッキングもありません。ページも単語も記録もiPadの中に残り、いつでも書き出せます。日本語、英語、スペイン語、ポルトガル語、フランス語に対応。

Apple Pencilがおすすめ。指でも書けます。

Character data (characters.kana)
================================

It is distributed under the same licences, and this attribution must be shown
to the app's users (an attribution / about screen).


KanjiVG

  Source used: release r20250816 (kanjivg-20250816.xml.gz).


KANJIDIC2
---------
Readings, meanings, school grades, JLPT levels, frequency ranks and radicals.

  This app uses the KANJIDIC2 dictionary file. This file is the property of the
  Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), and is used in
  conformance with the Group's licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
  4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Licence details: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
  Project page: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project


JLPT levels
-----------
The JLPT N5-N1 level of each kanji.

  From Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources, http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution ("BY"), as stated on
  http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/sharing/ ("use anything here however you like
  (commercial or non-commercial), but credit my site").
  These are community lists: since 2010 the JLPT publishes no official kanji
  lists, and the levels are Waller's best estimate.
